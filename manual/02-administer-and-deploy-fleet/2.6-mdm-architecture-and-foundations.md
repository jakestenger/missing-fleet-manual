# MDM architecture and foundations

Fleet coordinates the device-management protocol each supported platform provides. The Fleet server remains the control plane; the operating system’s MDM client receives profiles and commands through the platform-specific management path. This complements the fleetd and osquery channels used for host data and agent-side work.

## Choose the management boundary

Define which device populations Fleet will manage through MDM and which existing systems remain the authoritative owner for a capability. Apple, Windows, and Android each bring their own enrollment, identity, certificate, and service prerequisites. Linux has no equivalent platform MDM protocol, so its management path is agent and configuration-management based.

A clear boundary prevents duplicate profiles, competing software delivery, and ambiguous support ownership.

## Prepare shared prerequisites

MDM requires a stable public Fleet address, TLS, correctly exposed platform endpoints, and administrators who own platform credentials and renewal. Plan firewall and proxy paths before enrollment. Also decide who owns Apple Business Manager or Android enterprise administration, Windows enrollment configuration, certificates, tokens, and recovery.

## Connect one platform at a time

Establish the first platform connection, enroll a small pilot, and verify inventory, profile delivery, and a low-risk command. Only then introduce the next platform. The following chapters cover platform configuration; Part III covers device enrollment.

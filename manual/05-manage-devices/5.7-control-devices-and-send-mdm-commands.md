  A device action is defined by its operational consequence, but its prerequisites, point of no

release: Apple lock and wipe use Apple MDM; Windows lock and unlock and ALL Linux actions use

model rather than re-teaching it, but do say early that "MDM command" is not what several of these
actions are.

gives expected observations and a clear handoff threshold. Part VI and A.8 own exhaustive API and

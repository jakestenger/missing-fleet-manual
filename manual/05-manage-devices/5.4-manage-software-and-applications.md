THE ORGANISING MODEL, which the chapter must establish early and then use throughout:
the administrator's source choice cross-cuts three representation families and three delivery
channels, and it is that cross-cutting that explains every later exception.

  Custom package or Fleet-maintained app -> software_installers -> Orbit installer pipeline
  Apple App Store or Android Play app    -> vpp_apps            -> Apple MDM, or Android AMAPI
  In-house .ipa                          -> in_house_apps       -> Apple MDM

Android shares the App Store data model but neither VPP licensing nor Apple delivery. An .ipa
shares Apple delivery with App Store apps but not their representation. A Fleet-maintained app
shares the custom-package lifecycle rather than being a separate installer type. Teach this in
prose in the first section, as part of the source-comparison table. Do NOT organise the chapter
around database tables; Part VIII owns those, and an administrator does not think in them.

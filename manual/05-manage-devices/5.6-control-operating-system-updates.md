## Enforce Apple operating-system versions and deadlines

<!--
### macOS 14 and later
- Numeric target-version validation and Apple-availability validation.
- "Minimum" is the threshold deciding which hosts need action, while the generated declaration
  carries an EXACT TargetOSVersion. Do not let those collapse.
- Noon-local deadline. Native notification, deferral, forced installation, offline-at-deadline and
  restart all belong to the OS.
- The Rapid Security Response and build-suffix boundary: do not imply the numeric target expresses
  every Apple update identifier.

### macOS 13 and earlier
- fleetd and Nudge prerequisites, and the pipeline.
- THE DIFFERENT CLOCK: 20:00 UTC rather than noon local.
- What Nudge prompts or restricts against what macOS performs.
- What happens when the setting is removed, or fleetd cannot run Nudge.

### iOS and iPadOS
- Shared inputs, separate fleet settings, different user consequences, offline-at-deadline.
- No update_new_hosts equivalent.

### Update new Apple hosts during automated enrollment
- update_new_hosts semantics and the backward-compatible default.
- Below-minimum check against always-update when no minimum is configured.
- LATEST AVAILABLE version, not the configured target. Hand enrollment mechanics to Part III.
-->

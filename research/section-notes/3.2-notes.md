## Stated

| Claim | Source |
|---|---|
| Personal (BYOD) enrollment removes device lock and device erase from the profile's MDM access rights | `server/mdm/apple/apple_mdm.go`: `AppleEnrollmentAccessRights(personal)` returns `MDMAccessRightAll` for company-owned, and `MDMAccessRightAll &^ (MDMAccessRightDeviceLock | MDMAccessRightDeviceErase)` for personal |
| Company-owned carries thirteen rights, personal eleven | `MDMAccessRightAll = 8191 // all 13 bits (2^13 - 1)`, minus bits 2 and 3 |
| The distinction travels as a `byod` query parameter on the MDM server URL, is surfaced to the check-in handler, and lands on the host record | `FleetPersonalEnrollmentKey = "byod"`, with a source comment stating exactly this path and naming `host_mdm.is_personal_enrollment` |

## Derived

- **The two-channel framing** that opens the chapter is the book's own, built on 1.2's five channels. It is a teaching device, not a Fleet concept, and it is the reason this chapter is organised the way it is.
- **"Changing your mind means re-enrolling"** follows from the access rights being baked into the profile the device holds. Fleet exposes no way to change a delivered profile's access rights, and nothing was found that does; stated as a consequence rather than as a documented behaviour.
- **The ordering requirement for `--use-system-configuration`** (MDM, then profile, then package) follows from the package having no URL or secret of its own. Fleet does not document the ordering in those words.

## Unverified, and marked as such in the chapter

**What Fleet Free can actually do for macOS MDM enrollment.** Three licence facts are established above, and they do not add up to a supported matrix: the manual profile download and the Setup Assistant default are Premium, the OTA path is not gated. Whether a Free deployment is expected to enrol Macs by OTA, whether ADE is usable without Premium, and what Fleet intends here are **not documented anywhere I could find at this tag.** The chapter says so and tells the reader to confirm against their own instance rather than guessing, which is the honest handling of a licensing question the source does not settle.

This is the one place in the chapter where a reader could be badly misled by confident prose, so it is deliberately unconfident.

## Not claimed

- **Minimum supported macOS version.** Not established at the tag and not asserted.
- **Account-driven user enrollment**, which is iOS and iPadOS only at this release (Fleet rejects macOS with "only iOS and iPadOS devices are supported for account driven user enrollment"). Deliberately absent from the macOS path table; it belongs in 3.5.
- **UI click paths.** Per STYLE §6 the chapter describes the decision and the artifacts, not the console navigation, which is volatile.

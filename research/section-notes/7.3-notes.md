## Fleet reference deployment, tag checked

**Fleet's bundled Helm chart**, in its default configuration with an external database and automatic
migrations enabled, declares the migration as a pre-upgrade hook and **does not stop the existing
pods before it runs**. After the hook, the deployment is updated with no strategy specified.

The DigitalOcean example declares a pre-deploy migration job. The AWS example calls an external
module whose lifecycle is not in this repository.

**None of that is a Fleet guarantee**, which is the whole point of the class.

## Dependency and platform

The hook lifecycle is Helm's. The default rolling replacement, during which old and new pods overlap,
is Kubernetes's. **Corrected at review**, where I had attributed both to Fleet and had also compressed
two distinct periods, migration-time overlap and replacement-time overlap, into one.

## Operational practice

The preparation list, the rehearsal method for sizing a window, the before-and-after counts, the
declared rollback data-loss point, and the fleetd promotion rings are all local judgement.

**There is no duration model**, so rehearsal on production-shaped restored data plus measured drain,
replication, startup and acceptance time is the only defensible estimate. Fleet's published typical
duration is an observation of another deployment.

## Rejected, and why

**Two rollback rules in one chapter.** The early section said the way back after any migration is a
restore; the later section correctly allowed a binary rollback where compatibility is established.
One rule now runs through it, and it is the broader one: **is the current database state
backward-compatible with the old binary**, including what the new release has written, not only what
a migration removed.

**"Both are defensible."** Removed. It placed my judgement beside product evidence as though Fleet
had validated both paths.

**"The reference deployments do not do that."** Too collective. The chart's behaviour is
demonstrable; the other two need their own platforms checked.

# 1.3 — does a fleet inherit the global scope? — verification

Verified at **tag `fleet-v4.90.1`**, 2026-08-24. Written to settle a `VERIFY BEFORE SHIPPING`
marker left in 1.3 during the salvage merge, where the claim had been assembled from two
sources discussing different item types.

**Result: the asymmetry is real, and the draft had it the right way round.**

## Inherits: the global set applies alongside the fleet's

| Item | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Policies** | `CountMergedTeamPolicies` filters `(p.team_id = ? OR p.team_id IS NULL)`. The API also exposes an `inherited_policies` array on the team policies endpoint, added in 4.22.0 |
| **Reports** | `queries.go` applies `AND (team_id = ? OR team_id IS NULL)` |
| **Labels** | Label distribution filters `(team_id IS NULL OR team_id = ?)`, so a global label is evaluated on every host |

Corroborated by `articles/role-based-access.md`, which lists "View global (inherited)
reports and their results" and "View global (inherited) policies" as distinct fleet-level
permissions. The word *inherited* is Fleet's own.

## Does not inherit: exactly one scope matches

| Item | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Configuration profiles** | Source comment, verbatim: "EffectiveTeamID returns 0 for hosts not in a team. team_id=0 is its own team (the 'no team' / global scope), **NOT a fallback for teamed hosts**: a host with team_id=5 matches profiles with team_id=5 only, it does not also inherit team_id=0 profiles." |
| **Software** | Installers key on a single `global_or_team_id` column matched exactly, with no OR against the global value. VPP delivery uses null-safe equality on the team id, which is exact-match rather than a merge |

## Why it is worth stating plainly

The two halves generalise wrongly in both directions, which is what the chapter now says:
someone who learned the policy behaviour expects a global profile to reach everything, and
someone who learned the profile behaviour expects a global policy not to apply inside a
fleet. Both are wrong, and each is wrong about the other half.

## Not verified here
Scripts, OS settings, and setup experience were not checked. They are not claimed either way
in the chapter.

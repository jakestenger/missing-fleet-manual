First draft, 2026-08-27, against `fleet-v4.90.1` (dd0200f062).

## Derived

**"A finding is a conclusion from two inputs."** The chapter's organising claim. Each input is source-checked; framing the finding as their product, and observing that incompleteness in either shortens the list rather than erroring, is the manual's argument and the reason the chapter opens with the inventory switch rather than with vulnerabilities.

**The prioritisation order**, known-exploited then probability then reach then remediability, is editorial. The fields are source-checked; the ordering is judgement and is presented as such rather than as Fleet behaviour.

**"Version matching is the source of most false positives, and Linux distributions are where it bites."** The backporting problem is real and is why Fleet carries OVAL and goval data at all, but the word "most" is a frequency claim I have hedged into. **Flag for review**: either establish it or state the mechanism without the ranking.

## Deliberately not stated

**No claim about which feed covers which platform in detail.** The directory listing establishes that several sources exist; mapping each to its coverage needs a check per source that I have not done, and a wrong map here would be worse than the general statement that coverage differs.

**No claim about vulnerability history retention.** The agreed structure assigns exposure history to this chapter, and I have not verified the CVE dataset's retention or resolution. **Flag for review**: this is a named gap rather than an omission I am unaware of.

**No count of vulnerability sources.** The directory has entries that are not all feeds, so a count would be wrong in the way 4.1's detail-query count would have been.

## Boundaries held

Installing and updating software is Part V's, and the chapter hands over at the version rather than describing delivery. Patch policies are named and their trap repeated from 4.3, without re-explaining them. Single-host software absence goes to 8.4. Self-hosting concerns about feed reachability go to 2.9.

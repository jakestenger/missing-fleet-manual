## Derived

- That the API's enrichment plus the automation-author overwrite can jointly name a person on an automation row. Each half is verified in source; the interaction is the book's, and **has not been observed on a running instance**.
- That clearing `activity_host_past` makes previously-exempt activities eligible for expiry. Follows directly from the two verified queries, not stated by Fleet.
- The three-way attribution table as an incident heuristic.
- That a host-only event will never reach a SIEM. Follows from the streamer reusing the filtered query; Fleet does not warn about it anywhere found.

## Unverified

- The corrected `fleetctl api` and `curl | jq` commands are confirmed against the command surface and the response fields in source, but have **not been run against a 4.90.1 instance**.
- The compliance claim that Fleet does not sign or hash-chain activity rows remains a negative claim from an absence of evidence. Not re-verified this pass, and still stated in the text as an absence rather than a guarantee.
- The causal claims in the symptom-to-type table were not re-tested, in particular that editing a profile redelivers to every matching host.
- Authorization for the two activity endpoints is described from the policy layer's shape, not from a live permission test.

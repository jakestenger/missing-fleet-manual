## Derived

- That a partial archive reads as a complete one to the recipient, so omissions must be stated. Follows from the collector's continue-on-error behaviour; Fleet does not say it.
- That a truncated `db-process-list` is more misleading than a missing one. The behaviour of `SHOW PROCESSLIST` without `PROCESS` is MySQL's, not Fleet's, and was **not tested here**.
- The three escalate-early cases. Editorial.
- That the Orbit channel needs its own observation. Follows from 8.7's verified scope claim.

## Unverified

- The approved private customer-support route and severity terminology for this release. Fleet's public issue chooser does not answer it, and the chapter does not claim to.
- The expected Fleet status for Android Google-account-based enrollment in the intake list.
- The claim that VPP failures "expire on their own schedule". No responsible status or schedule is named, and none was located this pass.
- Whether every Premium feature fails quietly without a license key. Now marked as unverified in the text.
- The failure surface of a missing or changed `server_private_key`. Now marked as unverified in the text.

## Confirming review, 2026-08-27: NOT READY, now fully applied

Only one of the previous round's findings had actually been applied, and the confirming pass said so plainly: "the confirmation does not close cleanly ... the problem is incomplete application". This pass closed all five.

**Checklist items 3 and 4 were swapped, and the chapter claimed its numbering followed the source.** `mdm-bug-checklist.md` item 3 is serial numbers and other identifiers, item 4 is hardware model. The chapter had them the other way round while line 155 told the reader both sides could cite the same item number, which is the worst combination: wrong and asserted to be right. Swapped, and the note now also says which rows are this manual's additions with no counterpart in the source.

**iOS and iPadOS were missing three enrollment paths.** The table carried only account-driven user enrollment, but the tagged console offers iPhones and iPads a company-owned or personal choice on an enrollment URL (`IosIpadosPanel.tsx`, radio buttons defaulting to Personal, appending `byod=true`), and the DEP sync assigns them to their own fleets alongside macOS. Four rows now, marked as derived from the code and the console rather than quoted, because Fleet's checklist covers macOS, Windows and Android and stops there. A recipient who goes looking for them in the checklist will not find them, and the chapter says so.

**Google-account Android enrollment now refuses to guess.** The source names the method and supplies no status. The row previously read "unverified", which is honest but sits inside a column headed *MDM status Fleet should show* and so still reads as a prediction. The row now points at a paragraph that says to record what Fleet actually shows, and specifically not to reason from the two Android rows above it, which disagree with each other.

**Four overclaims, all of the same shape: a true observation carried one step further than the evidence goes.**

- The class-6 shortcut said UI-disagrees-with-host is "not a failure". 8.1 says the opposite once a refetch fails to clear it, at which point detail results are not being ingested and that is a real failure. Two chapters arguing.
- The VPP row said install failures "expire on their own schedule". Failures do not expire; the **token** does, on a clock independent of the APNs certificate and the ABM token, which is the fact worth having.
- "Premium-only features are not built" is wrong in a way that costs a support round trip. Fleet Free has **two** behaviours: explicit licence errors, which `fleetctl` returns for Premium MDM operations, and silent forcing-off, which the source states in those words for the low-disk-space filter (forced to nil) and software vulnerability detail (forced to false). The silent kind is the one that reads like a bug.
- **The worked example drew a conclusion from `cron_stats` that `cron_stats` cannot support.** It suggested `apple_mdm_apns_pusher` statistics would show whether particular hosts were woken. `SendPushesToPendingDevices` catches `APNSDeliveryError`, logs it at info level and **returns nil**, so a run in which Apple rejected the push for some of the batch records as a clean success. The row is green either way. Corrected in the example and then made explicit underneath, because a reader who has just been shown a model ticket will copy its reasoning.

**Where to file was one undifferentiated menu of thirteen templates**, most of which belong to Fleet's internal product process. It now opens with an audience routing table — support channel, bug report, reliability, ask-a-question, or whatever a Fleet employee named — and the template inventory carries a *Yours to open?* column marking eight of the thirteen as Fleet's. Blank issues being disabled is now stated as the reason you cannot escape the list rather than as a trailing fact.

**The universal header block was missing the fields Fleet's own template insists on**: actual behaviour, expected behaviour, reproduction steps, and reproduction status. Added, with the point that expected behaviour is the field people skip and the one the recipient cannot infer, and that the template's reproduction status is a two-way checkbox rather than a yes or no, so "describes the workflow but not reproduced on a second instance" is a complete answer that changes who picks the ticket up.

Also: "Gather every row" now defers to 8.13.1's urgent exceptions rather than contradicting them, and "Two notes" introduces three bullets no longer.

# 8.13 What to collect before escalating: citation ledger

Verified against `fleet-v4.90.1` (commit `dd0200f062`) in `~/Source/Fleet/fleet-public` on 2026-08-26.

The chapter's worked example, presented as the model of a well-written ticket, carried three errors. It is the passage most likely to be copied verbatim during an incident, which makes it the worst place in the chapter for them.

## Derived

- That a partial archive reads as a complete one to the recipient, so omissions must be stated. Follows from the collector's continue-on-error behaviour; Fleet does not say it.
- That a truncated `db-process-list` is more misleading than a missing one. The behaviour of `SHOW PROCESSLIST` without `PROCESS` is MySQL's, not Fleet's, and was **not tested here**.
- The three escalate-early cases. Editorial.
- That the Orbit channel needs its own observation. Follows from 8.7's verified scope claim.

## Unverified

- The approved private customer-support route and severity terminology for this release. Fleet's public issue chooser does not answer it, and the chapter does not claim to.
- The expected Fleet status for Android Google-account-based enrollment in the intake list.
- The claim that VPP failures "expire on their own schedule". No responsible status or schedule is named, and none was located this pass.
- Whether every Premium feature fails quietly without a license key. Now marked as unverified in the text.
- The failure surface of a missing or changed `server_private_key`. Now marked as unverified in the text.

## Open, not fixed here

The review's structural recommendation was to replace the flat intake inventory with a short first pass and conditional branches, and to lead with an audience-and-destination routing table. Not done, for the same reason as 8.12: it is a chapter-shape decision that should be taken across Part VIII rather than inside a correctness sweep.

## A check that was attempted and dropped

`nano_command_queue` was in prose, so `check-table-names.py` could not see it. Extending the checker to backticked snake_case identifiers in prose produced 37 false positives and 0 true positives across the manual, because Fleet spells config keys, MDM asset names, webhook names, activity types, and columns in the same shape. Similarity scoring does not separate them: the real error scores 0.774 against its nearest table while `vpp_token`, which is correct, scores 0.947. Reverted. Recorded in CONTRIBUTING.

# 2.7 Apple MDM configuration — research notes

Verified at **tag `fleet-v4.90.1`**, 2026-08-24. Autonomous run 3. Source:
`articles/apple-mdm-setup.md` (274 lines). Written from a 26-line outline to 192 lines.

## The organizing finding

**Connecting Fleet to Apple means holding four credentials, not one**, and they fail
differently. Three are renewed by hand; one renews itself, which is why it is the one people
forget exists.

| Credential | Lifetime | Renewed by | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| APNs push certificate | 1 year | You, via Apple | Commands stick in Pending; recovery is per-device |
| Apple Business token | expires | You | Automatic enrollment stops |
| Volume Purchasing token | expires | You | App Store delivery stops |
| Host SCEP certificates | 1 year | **Fleet, at 180 days** | Manually enrolled hosts lose MDM; user must re-enroll |

Fleet Premium gates Apple Business and Volume Purchasing. The push certificate does not.

## Two renewal traps, both verbatim from the guide

> When renewing, be sure to use the same Apple ID from year-to-year. If you don't, you will
> have to turn MDM off and back on for all Apple hosts.

> Make sure that the certificate's **Common Name (CN)** matches the one presented in Fleet. If
> you choose a different certificate, you must turn MDM off and back on for all Apple hosts.

They share a shape worth naming in the chapter: **the renewal appears to succeed and the
estate breaks afterwards.** No confirmation step catches either, which is why the CN check
belongs in the written procedure rather than in memory.

Fleet's own mitigation, quoted: use "a shared Apple Developer account to generate the APNs
certificate to make sure it can be renewed regardless of an employee's availability." That
sharpens the argument already made in 2.6.

## The expiry signature
> configuration profile changes and other MDM commands will remain stuck in "Pending" until
> renewal.

Diagnostically useful because Pending is also what an offline device looks like. A whole
estate in Pending while inventory still arrives is the certificate, not the devices.

## The stored-once default enrollment profile

The strongest non-obvious find. The default ADE profile is stored **once per Fleet instance**,
at first automatic-enrollment registration, and is "**not** refreshed by Fleet upgrades, by
adding or removing AB tokens, or by any other normal operation." There is no in-product reset
action.

Consequence stated in the chapter: **a fresh install and a long-running instance on the same
Fleet version can behave differently here**, and nothing surfaces it. Adopting newer defaults
means building a custom profile with those values.

## Other verified facts used

- Default fleet for automatically enrolled hosts is set **per platform** on the AB token; unset
  platforms land in Unassigned. A host can be moved to another fleet **before** it enrolls.
- **Re-enrolling an AB host clears its own stale state**: pending commands, script runs and
  software installs cancelled, completed ones cleared, labels reset. Deleting the host first is
  unnecessary.
- **That clearing is suppressed during MDM migration**, where labels and pending activity are
  preserved deliberately.
- Each VPP token corresponds to **one organization unit** in Apple Business, and is assigned to
  chosen fleets. Renew date column: yellow under 30 days, red once expired.
- One AB and one VPP token is the normal case. Fleet names four exceptions: MSPs (one per
  client), acquirers, umbrella organizations with separate purchasing authority, and
  international organizations.

## Terminology

Fleet now writes **Apple Business (AB)**. Earlier material, including this manual's 2.2
research, uses Apple Business Manager or ABM. The chapter notes the equivalence once so a
reader meeting either is not confused.

## Deliberately left out

- **Step-by-step Apple portal navigation.** Vendor UI, changes without notice, and §17 keeps
  volatile reference out of the narrative. The shape of each procedure is given; exact click
  paths are not.
- **Setup Assistant pane options.** Belongs with setup experience in Part V.
- **Per-country or per-entity token strategy detail.** The chapter names the four situations
  Fleet identifies and stops there; the multi-org guidance is long and reads as consulting
  advice rather than product behaviour.
- **SCEP renewal internals.** The 180-day cadence and the failure consequence are stated; the
  mechanism was not traced.

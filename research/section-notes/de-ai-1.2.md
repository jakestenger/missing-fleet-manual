## Judgement calls worth a second opinion

- **Kept one negative contrast in the opening**: "there is no process called `fleetd`".
  Administrators do go looking for that process in `ps` and Task Manager, which is the case
  §15 reserves explicit contrast for. It is now a trailing clause rather than a sentence.
- **Kept "only" in three places** where it carries the meaning rather than a defensive tone:
  the heading "What only Fleet Desktop can do", "the only end-user-facing surface fleetd
  has", and the platform-scope uses ("macOS only", "Orbit plus osquery only").
- **Kept the negative capability columns** in the "You get / You do not get" table and the
  "no / yes" platform matrix. They are reference tables where the absent capability is the
  fact the reader came for.
- **Left the five-channels content as tables plus per-channel prose**, rather than the full
  narrative rewrite Jake's comment sketched. Each channel now has its own paragraph, but the
  endpoint, receiver and throttle listings stayed tabular; turning those into prose would
  cost a reader scanning for an endpoint. Worth confirming that split is what he wanted.
- **Corrected an inaccuracy while rewording**, flagged here because it is a fact change and
  not a style change: the old gloss on the online-host formula read "within the last 60
  seconds", which understates the window the formula computes. It now reads "within its own
  check-in interval plus a minute".

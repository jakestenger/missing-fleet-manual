## Derived

Reasoning the chapter does over verified facts. Fleet does not state these.

- That raising log verbosity is a different act from reading logs, and so does not conflict with 8.1's "logs are step seven". The underlying facts (DEBUG-level per-request lines, no retroactive logging) are verified; the reconciliation is the book's.
- That a control narrows an investigation without closing a branch. Editorial.
- That a stale one-host label will quietly narrow someone else's change later. Consequence of manual label membership persisting, not a documented failure mode.
- That a hostname which matches no host yields an empty label rather than an error. Follows from the identifier being deprecated rather than rejected; **not separately tested**.
- The reading of the differential table generally, and the caution that a platform-only failure makes a platform-path cause likely rather than certain.

## Unverified

Carried forward. None of these are asserted in the chapter as fact.

- The env var spellings `FLEET_MDM_APPLE_DEP_SYNC_PERIODICITY` and `FLEET_SERVER_VPP_VERIFY_REQUEST_DELAY` are inferred from Fleet's config-key convention. The keys and defaults are verified; the spellings are not. The chapter's own "What is not documented" table says so.
- The behaviour of `sudo profiles renew -type enrollment` is Apple's, and Fleet's repository cannot establish it.
- The Windows 480 minute relaxed poll and 1 minute fast poll are carried from 8.9 and were verified there, not re-verified here.
- The revised Compose recipe has not been run end to end from an empty directory. The two edits it prescribes are verified against the published files; the resulting stack was not started.
- Every row of the force-action table in §8.11.6 is behavioural and was not re-tested at 4.90.1.

## Rejected from the review

- **Remove the `[!internal]` callout.** It is a manual-wide convention across Part VIII, used in thirteen chapters to carry support-history context. The reviewer had no way to know that.
- **Remove the "What is not documented" table as publication scaffolding.** The table tells a reader which claims not to lean on, which is worth more in a troubleshooting chapter than a clean surface. The incorrect row in it was fixed rather than the table dropped.
- **Restructure the chapter into a single incident workflow.** 8.1 owns the procedure; this chapter owns the techniques it calls. Duplicating the sequence here would give the reader two orderings to reconcile, which is the failure mode that produced the finding in the first place. The opening now hands off to 8.1 explicitly instead.

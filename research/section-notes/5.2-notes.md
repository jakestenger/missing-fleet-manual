# 5.2 Manage configuration profiles and declarative settings, citation ledger

Drafted 2026-08-27 against `fleet-v4.90.1` (dd0200f062).

## Stated

| Claim | Source at the tag |
|---|---|
| Profile delivery status is one of pending, verifying, verified, failed, or unset | `server/fleet/mdm.go`, the `MDMDeliveryStatus` constants and `IsValid` |
| Verifying means the command applied but Fleet has not independently verified it | Same file, the doc comment block above those constants, quoted almost directly |

## Derived

**The three-step choice** (Fleet's own settings, then a declaration, then a custom profile) is
editorial. The supporting fact is the OS updates guard, which shows Fleet actively defending one
such boundary; the general ordering is my recommendation.

**"Removal is automatic."** Follows necessarily from scope being a continuously evaluated
membership test, and from the presence of a remove operation type. Stated as a consequence.

**"Uploading is not validating."** Editorial framing of the boundary between what Fleet checks and
what it cannot.

**The Apple identifier collision point** is general Apple platform behaviour rather than Fleet
behaviour, and is stated as a property of the device.

**The diagnostic order** at the end is editorial method.

## Deliberately not stated

**No profile format tutorial.** Writing a property list payload or a Windows configuration path is
the platform vendor's documentation, not Fleet's, and it would date. The chapter covers what Fleet
does with the document.

**No claim about how Fleet performs its independent verification, or on which platforms.** The
existence of the verifying-versus-verified distinction is established for all three platforms by
the shared status table. 8.6 describes the verification pass as an Apple mechanism. Whether Windows
and Android reach `verified` by the same route was not established. The chapter states what the
states mean and does not describe the mechanism. **Flag for a later round.**

**No retry timing.** The retry counts are established; how long Fleet waits between attempts was
not checked.

**No enumeration of which settings have built-in Fleet controls.** That list belongs with each
subject: encryption in 5.8, OS updates in 5.6.

## Boundaries held

Disk encryption is 5.8, OS updates 5.6, scripts 5.3, software 5.4. MDM channel diagnosis stays in
8.8 and 8.9, and the SQL-level account of profile status stays in 8.6. The desired state model and
the four channels are established in 5.1 and cited rather than repeated.

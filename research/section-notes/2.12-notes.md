## Deferred to other chapters

- **Android password policy quirk on BYOD.** Google reinterprets `passwordMinimumLength` on personally owned devices: 8 means PIN 8 / password 6, and 6 means PIN 6 / password 4. Source: `docs/solutions/android/configuration-profiles/README.md`. Belongs in the configuration profiles chapter, not here.
- **Pub/Sub callback token rotation.** Recorded above; belongs with the security discussion rather than setup.

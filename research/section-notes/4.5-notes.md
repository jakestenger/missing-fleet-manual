# 4.5 Monitor fleet-wide state, citation ledger

First draft, 2026-08-27, against `fleet-v4.90.1` (dd0200f062). Re-scoped from the outline's "Monitor device and management status" to fleet-wide monitoring, agreed with the reviewer before drafting because the original scope duplicated 1.2, 4.1 and 8.1.

## Stated

| Claim | Source at the tag |
|---|---|
| The host summary carries total, online, offline, missing-in-action, new, low disk space, a per-platform breakdown and an automated-enrollment error count | `server/fleet/hosts.go`, the `HostSummary` struct |

## Derived

**The four posture questions** and the recurring review sequence are editorial. Each underlying signal is source-checked; ordering them into a repeatable review is the manual's contribution and is presented as advice rather than as Fleet behaviour.

**"Turn an observation into a label."** Editorial, and it follows from 1.3's account of what labels are for.

**"A Premium capability presenting as an absence rather than a refusal."** The low-disk nulling is source-checked. Calling it a pattern is derived, though the manual has now met it enough times to be confident: 8.13 records the same shape for other settings.

## Deliberately not stated

**No claim about which filters exist on the host list.** The interface offers several and enumerating them from source would produce a list that drifts; the chapter names the categories and does not claim completeness.

**No CSV or pagination limits.** Both exist; establishing the numbers needs a check I have not done, and a wrong limit is worse than none.

**No recommended webhook percentage or day count.** The settings are source-checked; a recommended value would be a claim about the reader's estate, which STYLE 25 forbids.

**Flag for review:** the summary struct also carries a separate thirty-day missing count alongside the missing-in-action count, and I have not established how the two differ in practice. The chapter names only one of them rather than guessing at the distinction.

## Additions

Two, both filling a gap rather than correcting an error.

### 1. The `/debug/` gate has two conditions, and the chapter documented one

**Stated**, `server/service/debug_handler.go`. The middleware rejects unless `v.CanPerformActions()` **and** the user's global role is admin. The chapter described only the role half.

`CanPerformActions` is false when the user is not logged in or when `IsAdminForcedPasswordReset()` is true. So a genuine global admin whose password an administrator has just reset gets the same HTTP 403 with the same one-word `Unauthorized` body as a team admin would. Two different causes, one indistinguishable response, on a route family whose whole purpose is diagnosis.

Added, because the diagnostic move is not obvious: the thing to check is the account's password-reset flag, not the token or the role.

### 2. The trace sampler's route tiers

**Stated**, the 4.87.0 changelog entry, which is precise: agent firehose endpoints (osquery distributed read and write, Orbit ping and config, device desktop and ping) at 0.1%; admin reads at 2%; everything else, including enroll, SCEP, MDM check-in, cron jobs and GitOps batch, at 100%; liveness probes (`/healthz`, `/version`, `/metrics`) dropped unconditionally.

The chapter named the two ratio settings and mentioned 0.1% in passing without saying which routes sit where. The tiering is the operationally useful half: it tells a reader that a missing agent check-in trace is expected sampling rather than a missing request, and that a missing enrollment trace is a real absence. Added with that reading spelled out.

Also tightened "about once a minute" to the 60 seconds the source states.

## Not verifiable at this tag, and correctly marked in the chapter

- **`block.prof` and `mutex.prof` come back empty.** The chapter's reasoning is that Go collects these only once a program sets a sampling rate and Fleet never does. Absence of a call is weaker evidence than presence of one, and the chapter states the conclusion as expected behaviour rather than as a Fleet setting, which is the right register. Left as **derived**.
- **The 30-second CPU profile timing out from a remote client.** The chapter explicitly labels this inferred from timeout arithmetic rather than documented, and names the candidates (ingress, proxy, load balancer idle). Correct as marked.
- **The support-corpus figures in §8.5.8** (zero appearances across 5,112 entries) come from Jake's own field work, not from Fleet's source, and are covered by STYLE §25's exception for his experience. Not checkable here and not treated as if it were.
- `go tool pprof` and `go tool trace` invocations are Go toolchain behaviour.
